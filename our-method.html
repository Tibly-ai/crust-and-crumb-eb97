The Journey of a Loaf

Every morning we wake early to bake. Here is how simple grains become the deeply flavorful, deeply textured crusts you find on our shelves.

01

Stoneground Grains

We source identity-preserved wheat directly from regional farmers. The grain is stoneground fresh, retaining the essential germ and bran where the deepest grain flavors, vitamins, and minerals live.

02

Slow Wild Rise

Our wild starter digests gluten and starches over a 36-hour slow, cold fermentation. This lengthy timeline breaks down hard-to-digest proteins, resulting in a complex tang and an easily digestible loaf.

03

Stone Hearth Bake

We hand-shape each loaf before baking it on blazing-hot stone hearths. Steam introduced in the first minutes of baking yields a crisp, deep-colored caramelized blistered crust with a soft, open interior.
