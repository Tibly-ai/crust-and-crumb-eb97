Baked Fresh Daily

Morning bakes straight from our hearth

Every loaf we bake starts with pure spring water, sea salt, and our active wild yeast starter. We ferment our doughs slowly for over 24 hours to coax out complex flavors, a deep golden crust, and a tender, open crumb.
