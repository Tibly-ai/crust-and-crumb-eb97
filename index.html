Our Three-Day Flour Journey

We never rush our dough. Beautiful texture, proper hydration, and nutritious loaves take exactly seventy-two hours from raw mill flour to the final bake.

01

Milling & Mixing

We select organic grains sourced from farms in the Pacific Northwest, freshly milled to preserve natural nutrients and grain aroma.

02

30-Hour Fermentation

The mixed dough rests in cold cellar temperatures for over thirty hours. This slow-growth environment breaks down complex starches.

03

Hand Shaping

Each batch is divided, weighed, and gently shaped by hand into circular boules or long batards before overnight proofing.

04

Stone Hearth Bake

Baked hot inside our high-heat stone deck oven, complete with steam injection to create our signature thick, dark, caramelized crust.
